## 🎯 DOCUMENTARY PHILOSOPHY

**What This Captures:**

1. **Pain:** Don't hide the homelessness, the mental hospital, the family rejection
2. **Faith:** This is a story about trusting God when you have nothing else
3. **Obsession:** 12-hour days for 21 days isn't normal. That's okay.
4. **Isolation:** Choosing to be alone to create something beautiful
5. **Redemption:** From scamming bookies at 16 to building legitimate systems at 23
6. **Craft:** The technical work matters, but it's in service of something bigger
7. **Honesty:** "I'm not a scammer anymore" is the most important line
8. **Humility:** "I'm still a guest. I'm still learning."

**What This Doesn't Do:**

- Glorify the scamming (acknowledge it, learn from it, move past it)
- Blame the family (they didn't understand, that's okay)
- Ask for pity (this isn't a sob story, it's a victory story)
- Hide the struggle (show the real low points)
- Pretend it was easy (it was brutal)
- Over-explain the technical stuff (let the work speak)
- Rush the emotional moments (let them land)

## 🎬 FILMING NOTES

### **Visual Style:**
- Handheld, intimate, sometimes shaky (it's raw)
- Natural light always
- Never glamorize the struggle
- Show the messy apartment
- Show the 3 AM coding sessions
- Show the empty phone
- Show the isolation

### **Pacing:**
- Slow. Let moments breathe.
- Don't rush past the pain.
- Don't rush past the wins.
- Trust the audience to feel it.

### **Narration:**
- First person, always
- Raw emotion, not performed
- Sometimes choked up (that's real)
- Sometimes defiant (that's also real)
- Never apologizing (this is the truth)

### **Interviews:**
- Keep them sparse
- Barista, roommate, old friend
- Show the voicemail/texts from family (brief, painful)
- Don't interview people who doubted (their doubt speaks for itself)

### **Music:**
- Minimal in narration
- Let the words carry weight
- Use music for montages
- Use silence for heavy moments
