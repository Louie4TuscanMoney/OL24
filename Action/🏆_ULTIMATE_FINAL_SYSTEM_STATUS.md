## 🔥 WHAT ISOTONIC CALIBRATION DOES

### **Problem:**
Models predict values, but they're not perfectly calibrated.  
Example: When model predicts +8, actual might be +9 or +7 (systematic bias).

### **Solution:**
Isotonic regression learns the mapping: predicted → actual  
It corrects systematic biases without changing model.

### **Result:**
```
Before calibration:
  Bayesian: 5.352 / 9.951 MAE

After calibration:
  Bayesian + Isotonic: 5.181 / 9.655 MAE
```
