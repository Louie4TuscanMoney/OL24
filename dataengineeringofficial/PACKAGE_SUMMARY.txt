PAPER DETAILS:
--------------

DEJAVU (Kang et al., 2020)
- Authors: Kang (Beihang), Spiliotis (NTUA), Petropoulos (Bath), et al.
- Method: K-NN pattern matching (Euclidean/DTW/Correlation distance)
- Dataset: M1/M3 (3,830 series), M4 reference (95,000 series)
- Performance: Yearly MASE 2.783 (BEST), Monthly MASE 0.932
- Best for: ≤6 years of data, short sequences (18 timesteps)
- NBA fit: ✅ Perfect (18-minute patterns, instant deployment)

CONFORMAL (Schlembach et al., 2022)
- Authors: Schlembach (Maastricht), Smirnov, Koprinska (Sydney)
- Method: Weighted quantiles + Bonferroni correction
- Dataset: ELEC2 (1,650 examples, t=192, h=12)
- Performance: 90% coverage guaranteed (even under distribution shifts)
- Best for: Calibrated uncertainty, model-agnostic wrapper
- NBA fit: ✅ Perfect (handles momentum swings, wraps Mamba)

INFORMER (Zhou et al., 2021)
- Authors: Zhou (Beihang), Zhang (Berkeley), Peng, Zhang (AWS), et al.
- Method: ProbSparse Self-Attention (O(L log L) complexity)
- Dataset: ETT (12/4/4 months), ECL (15/3/4 months), Weather (28/10/10 months)
- Performance: ETTh1-720 MSE 0.540 vs LSTM 1.511 (64.3% improvement)
- Best for: Long sequences (300+ timesteps), far horizons (96+ steps)
- NBA fit: ⚠️ Overkill (needs 300+ inputs, NBA has 18)

MATHEMATICAL FOUNDATIONS:
-------------------------
Dejavu:     K-NN pattern matching (Euclidean, DTW, correlation)
            Weighted forecast: y_hat = sum(w_i * y_i) / sum(w_i)

Conformal:  Weighted quantiles: Q_{1-α}({(r_i, w_i)})
            Bonferroni correction: α_corrected = α / h
            Exponential weighting: w_i = exp(-λ * (n - i))

Informer:   ProbSparse: M(q_i) = ln(sum(exp(q_i * k_j / sqrt(d)))) - 1/L * sum(q_i * k_j / sqrt(d))
            Top-u queries: Q_sparse = top_u(M(q_i), u = c * ln L)
            Complexity: O(L ln L) vs O(L^2)
