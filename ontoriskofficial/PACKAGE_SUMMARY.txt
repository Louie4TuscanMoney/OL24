🎯 WHAT IS ONTORISK?
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

DEFINITION:
OntoRisk is an advanced risk management framework that transforms ML predictions
into optimal bet sizes using probability calibration, Kelly Criterion, and
game-specific risk adjustments.

ONE-LINE SUMMARY:
ML Prediction → Probability Calibration → Edge Calculation → Kelly Sizing →
Risk Management → Optimal Bet Size

PURPOSE:
Bridge the gap between "I think Team A will win" and "I should bet $X on Team A"

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
🧠 THE FIVE PHASES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

PHASE 1: PROBABILITY CALIBRATION
Purpose: ML prediction → True win probability
Problem: ML models are overconfident (60% prediction ≠ 60% real win rate)
Solution: Sigmoid calibration based on model MAE
Result: Accurate win probabilities

PHASE 2: MODEL INTEGRATION
Purpose: Connect any ML model to OntoRisk
Problem: Different models output different formats
Solution: Unified adapter interface
Result: Works with Mamba, scikit-learn, XGBoost, etc.

PHASE 3: HISTORICAL SPREADS
Purpose: Analyze market movements
Problem: Need closing line value (CLV) and sharp indicators
Solution: Track opening/closing lines, line movement
Result: Validate predictions against market

PHASE 4: RISK MANAGEMENT
Purpose: Prevent ruin, manage bankroll
Problem: Kelly can be too aggressive
Solution: Hard limits (5% max bet, 20% daily exposure, -20% stop loss)
Result: Sustainable long-term growth

PHASE 5: ARCHETYPE CLASSIFICATION
Purpose: Adjust sizing by game type
Problem: Not all games have equal risk
Solution: Classify games (Toss-Up, Blowout, High Total, etc.)
Result: Reduced bets on risky games

🔑 KEY CONCEPTS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. PROBABILITY CALIBRATION
   Raw prediction → Calibrated probability
   Example: 60% → 57% (adjusted for model overconfidence)

2. EDGE CALCULATION
   Edge = Your Probability - Implied Probability
   Example: 55% - 52.38% = +2.62% edge
   Rule: Need >2% edge to bet

3. KELLY CRITERION
   f* = (bp - q) / b
   Maximizes long-term bankroll growth
   Use fractional (25%-50%) for safety

4. FRACTIONAL KELLY
   Full Kelly = Max growth, high variance
   Half Kelly = 75% growth, half variance
   Quarter Kelly = 50% growth, quarter variance
   Recommended: 25%-50% Kelly

5. RISK OF RUIN
   RoR = ((1 - Edge) / (1 + Edge)) ^ (Bankroll / Avg Bet)
   Goal: Keep RoR < 1%
   Solution: Bet smaller or higher edge only

6. GAME ARCHETYPES
   Toss-Up (≤3): 1.0x Kelly
   Moderate (3-7): 1.0x Kelly
   Heavy (7-12): 0.8x Kelly
   Blowout (>12): 0.5x Kelly (garbage time risk)

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
🛡️ RISK MANAGEMENT RULES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

HARD LIMITS (NEVER VIOLATED):
✅ Max single bet: 5% of current bankroll
✅ Max daily exposure: 20% of bankroll
✅ Minimum edge: 2% (to overcome vig + variance)
✅ Stop loss: Stop betting at -20% drawdown
✅ Minimum bankroll: $500

SOFT LIMITS (ADJUSTED BY ARCHETYPE):
✅ Fractional Kelly: 0.25x-0.5x (never full Kelly)
✅ Blowout games: 0.5x multiplier
✅ High total games: 0.9x multiplier
✅ Back-to-back games: 0.8x multiplier
✅ Low confidence: 0.5x multiplier
